## Binding conclusions

**Privy:** It can create Stellar wallets and sign Stellar transaction hashes according to official documentation. Calling it “unsupported on Stellar” would be incorrect. Classic envelope signing is technically compatible; SEP-10 sequence-zero challenges, fee-bump handling, Soroban authorization payloads and LendWise recovery/session integration have not been demonstrated. Do not equate Tier 2 with managed broadcasting, Stellar gas sponsorship or complete Soroban policy support.

**Mercuryo:** Native Stellar USDC and XLM are verified provider offerings, including card/payment-wallet purchase journeys. Availability for a new LendWise widget, exact asset issuer, countries, limits and refunds requires partner validation. No verified single atomic XLM+USDC funding operation exists in this review. Promotional fees/limits are expired and must not be reused.

**Etherfuse:** Public current prices, rates and Stellar balances are feasible now. Do not label `current_basis_points / 10000` as effective APY until its day-count, compounding and fee treatment are confirmed. Public snapshots cannot prove historic realized returns or investor maturity. Subscription/redemption interfaces exist, but public API docs are not contractual integration permission.

**History and positions:** Spiko is the strongest additional history candidate because an official public historical API is documented. Etherfuse can reliably build prospective history; a retrospective archive needs confirmation. Franklin publishes yield/NAV information but unrestricted machine-readable collection was not established; Ondo, WisdomTree and Centrifuge history remains unresolved for this design. Etherfuse Classic trustlines, correctly mapped BENJI Classic holdings, and validated Spiko contract balances can be observed publicly; legal ownership, accrued entitlements and redemption rights may require private account servicing data.

**Private access:** Etherfuse order/customer APIs, Spiko investor/distributor access, issuer servicing integrations, Privy tenant credentials and Mercuryo merchant configuration require credentials or agreements. Public ledger access does not remove those requirements. API availability and legal permission remain separate gates.
