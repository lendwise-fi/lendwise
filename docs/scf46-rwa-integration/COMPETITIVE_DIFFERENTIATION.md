# Competitive differentiation and originality review

**Do not claim LendWise is the first RWA yield aggregator.** RWA discovery, token/fund classification, history and API distribution already exist. The defensible hypothesis is that an existing lending platform can help users compare investment conditions and track compatible holdings across lending and Stellar RWAs with transparent financial semantics. That hypothesis needs a working product and consumer evidence.

## Claims to remove or qualify

Remove “first,” “risk-free,” “guaranteed leveraged profit,” “all RWAs directly investable,” “instant redemption” without exact product evidence, and “automatic eligibility via wallet sign-in.” Do not say market coverage means wallet/execution coverage or use issuer network-wide AUM as LendWise activity. Do not claim a completed smart-contract audit, formal SCF #45 validation or commercial partnerships without their evidence.

Proposed positioning: **LendWise will extend its lending data and wallet infrastructure with Etherfuse on Stellar, preserving financial conventions and investor conditions so users and applications can understand which opportunities are actually comparable.** This is a planned behavior, not a demonstrated production capability or exclusive competitive feature.

Measure product differentiation through task-based user tests: explain why a higher MXN headline rate is not a USD profit forecast, identify a stale NAV, understand restricted transfer rights, recover an interrupted funding journey, and obtain usable typed data in an external application. These tests establish utility more credibly than a feature checklist or an unsupported claim of market leadership.
